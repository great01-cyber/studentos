# studentos
Building an operating system for students.

According to Ascend Education (2025), "Picture this: You wake up, check your LMS for announcements, open your email for assignment notifications, hop onto a Zoom lecture, follow up with discussion boards on yet another platform, and finally try an AI tutor to clarify doubts. By the time lunch rolls around, your brain feels fried not from learning, but from juggling too many digital tools. This is the reality for countless students today. The rise of EdTech has revolutionized education, making learning more accessible and flexible than ever. But there’s a growing downside: EdTech burnout. While digital platforms promise convenience and personalized learning, overexposure can leave students mentally exhausted, overwhelmed, and questioning whether all this technology is truly helping." Therefore research shows students are overwhelmed by fragmented digital tools.
Studies report anxiety, technostress, exhaustion, and productivity loss caused by juggling multiple platforms.” (Sillence et al., 2023; Tafesse et al., 2024; Ascend Education, 2025; Drisya, 2023)
